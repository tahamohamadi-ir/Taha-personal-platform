# Logo redesign concepts — selection round 01

These three raster concepts are exploratory references, not production logos.
After selection, the chosen direction must be redrawn as precise SVG geometry,
tested in one color, and exported as favicon, header mark and social avatar.

## Option 01 — Portal Monogram

- File: `option-01-portal-monogram.png`
- Idea: interlocking `t` and `m`, architectural arch, stairs and passage into knowledge.
- Strength: strongest connection to the portal/architecture language of the site.
- Refinement need: simplify the interior and remove small stair details for favicon use.

## Option 02 — Research Constellation

- File: `option-02-research-constellation.png`
- Idea: `T`/`M` monogram crossed by a compact research network.
- Strength: research and connected disciplines read immediately.
- Refinement need: reduce literal letterforms and make the silhouette more ownable.

## Option 03 — Human Signal

- File: `option-03-human-signal.png`
- Idea: continuous human-centered signal rising through a `T`/`M` structure.
- Strength: premium, human and future-facing character.
- Refinement need: remove serif-like details and improve recognition at small sizes.

## Generation mode

Created with the built-in image generation tool using three independent
`logo-brand` prompts. Each prompt required a standalone transparent-background
mark, a navy/turquoise system with a restrained accent, no wordmark, no mockup,
and a silhouette that can be reconstructed as vector artwork.

## Selection rule

Choose the underlying idea, not the raster finish. The selected concept will be
rebuilt with a fixed grid, optical corrections, dark/light and monochrome
variants, safe-area rules, minimum-size tests and SVG/PNG exports.
